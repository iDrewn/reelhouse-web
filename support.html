Help with Biokoll

Found a bug or have an idea? Open an issue on GitHub. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, which cinema you had selected and what you were doing.

Common questions

Which cinemas does it cover?

Five cinemas in southern Sweden: the ones in Laholm, Ljungby, Markaryd, Oskarström and Strömsnäsbruk. Pick one from the map-pin button at the top right, or in the Cinemas tab. Your choice is remembered.

Is Biokoll an official app of the cinemas?

No. Biokoll is an independent app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by any cinema or booking provider. It shows the schedule the cinemas publish on their own website.

How do I buy a ticket?

Open a film, then tap the time you want. Biokoll asks for that showing’s ticket link and opens the cinema’s own ticket page inside the app, where you pick seats and pay. Payment is handled entirely by the cinema’s ticket system, and Biokoll never sees your details. If a showing can’t be booked online, the app says so and offers to call the cinema.

Why are the film descriptions in Swedish when the app is in English?

The cinemas only publish descriptions in Swedish, so that is what the app can show. Dates, times, genres, age ratings and every button follow the language you choose in the Cinemas tab.

How do I change the language?

Open the Cinemas tab and pick Svenska or English under Language. The app starts in Swedish if your iPhone is set to Swedish, and in English otherwise.

How do the day and age filters work?

The chips under the title filter the Now showing tab. Tap a day to see only that day’s films, each with that day’s times, or use the calendar button for a date that isn’t in the row. The Age chip picks all ages, under 15, or 15 and over, and it applies to Coming soon as well. If nothing matches, tap “Clear filters”. With no filter picked you see everything, as before.

Why is a film under “No age rating listed”?

Some films, especially upcoming ones, have no age rating yet. Rather than guess, Biokoll puts them in their own section below the films that match your filter.

What is Coming soon?

Films with an announced premiere date that aren’t on the schedule yet. Once a film gets scheduled shows it moves to Now showing. Past premiere dates are left out.

How do the trailers work?

On a film with a trailer, tap the play button on the poster or the one in the Trailer section. The video plays in place using YouTube’s embedded player. Nothing from YouTube is loaded until you tap play. If a trailer won’t play, use the “Open in YouTube” link under it.

Can I use it without a connection?

Yes, for what you have already looked at. Biokoll keeps the last schedule it loaded and the posters on your phone. When it can’t refresh, it shows the saved schedule with a note saying when it was last updated. Booking and trailers need a connection.

The times look wrong.

Times are shown in Swedish local time wherever you are. If a time is wrong compared with the cinema’s own site, pull down to refresh, and if it still differs please open an issue.

Does it work on iPad?

Biokoll is made and tested for iPhone. It may run on iPad, but the layout hasn’t been checked there.
