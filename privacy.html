Privacy policy

Biokoll Småland · Last updated 14 October 2026

Biokoll Småland does not collect, store or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no third-party tracking, and it does not send anything about you to the developer.

What stays on your phone

Biokoll Småland remembers which cinema you picked and which language you chose, and it keeps a copy of the last schedule it loaded and of the posters and cast photos you have seen, so the app works without a connection. This is stored on your iPhone only and is removed when you delete the app.

What the app connects to

To show you what is on, Biokoll Småland makes network requests. Like any request to a web server, these let the server see your IP address. The app sends no account, device or advertising identifier with them.

  • The cinemas’ schedule service (laganbio.se), for the list of cinemas, the shows and the film details, and, when you tap a time, for that one showing’s ticket link. The request contains only which cinema or which showing you are looking at.
  • Image servers (cdn.bio24.se for posters and image.tmdb.org for cast photos), to load the pictures.
  • YouTube (youtube-nocookie.com), only when you tap play on a trailer. The video is then played by YouTube’s embedded player, and what YouTube collects while it plays is covered by Google’s privacy policy. Nothing from YouTube is loaded before you tap.
  • The cinema’s ticket system, when you open a ticket page. It opens in an in-app browser and is run by the ticketing company, under its own privacy policy. Biokoll Småland does not see what you enter there.

Permissions

Biokoll Småland does not ask for access to your location, photos, contacts, camera or microphone. The Call, Email and Map buttons hand off to the Phone, Mail and Maps apps, which are then governed by Apple’s own policies.

Children

Biokoll Småland does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated date.
