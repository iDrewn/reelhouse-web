Biokoll Småland

What’s on at your local cinema, what’s coming, and the trailer, right on the page. Scroll and the reel spins.

In development · iPhone · iOS 26

Screenshots use fictional films, posters and cinemas.

The whole programme, in your pocket

Pick your cinema and see what’s on, find a time that suits you and go.

Now showing

Everything on at your cinema

Posters, times and details for the shows coming up, soonest first. Switch between five cinemas in southern Sweden with one tap.

Filters

Pick a day, pick an age

Choose any day to see that day’s films with their times, or show only films for under 15 or for 15 and over. Films without an age rating are listed separately, never guessed.

Coming soon

Premieres, by month

Films with a premiere date that aren’t on the schedule yet, grouped by month, so you know what to look forward to.

Trailers

Right on the page

Tap play on the poster and the page scrolls to the trailer and starts it. It plays in place, with no jump out of the app.

Tickets

From the cinema itself

Tap a time and the cinema’s own ticket page opens inside the app. Biokoll Småland never sees or handles your payment.

Languages

Svenska and English

Switch in one tap. Dates, times and labels follow along. Film descriptions come from the cinema and stay in Swedish.

Offline

Saved for later

The latest schedule and the posters are kept on your phone, so you can check what’s on even without a connection.

Private

No accounts, no tracking

No sign-up, no ads and no analytics. Read the privacy policy.

A look inside

Built for iOS 26 with the Liquid Glass design. Shown on an iPhone 18 Pro simulator, with fictional films.

The details

What is in the app today.

Screens

  • Now showing: a poster grid with the next show on each film
  • Coming soon: premieres grouped by month
  • Movie page: showtimes by day, plot, director, cast and the trailer
  • Cinemas: call, email or open the map, and switch language

Filters

  • Day: the days with shows, or any date from a calendar
  • Age: all ages, under 15, or 15 and over
  • Films without an age rating appear in their own labelled section
  • One tap clears the filters, and the default view shows everything

Good to know

  • Saved schedule and posters for use without a connection
  • Times are Swedish local time
  • Light and dark appearance
  • Trailers play from YouTube’s embedded player, only when you tap play

Requirements

  • iPhone with iOS 26 or later
  • An internet connection to refresh the schedule
  • Not on the App Store yet
